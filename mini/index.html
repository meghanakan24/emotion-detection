Tips for Better Accuracy

  • Hold each expression for 2–3 seconds — results are smoothed over time
  • Exaggerate expressions slightly (especially Disgust, Surprise, Angry)
  • Face the camera directly with even front lighting
  • Disgust is the hardest — wrinkle your nose and raise your upper lip clearly
  • Surprise vs Fear: Surprise = wide eyes + open mouth; Fear = tense, no smile

This model was trained on low-res 48×48 images. It works best for Happy/Neutral and struggles with rare emotions like Disgust.
